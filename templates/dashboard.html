Trend & typical performance
About these indicators

EMA gives recent videos more weight, starting from the first full-window mean. Median shows the middle value in each window, reducing the influence of viral spikes. Relative20 compares each video with the mean of the previous 20, excluding itself: 1.5× means 50% above that baseline. Zero baselines have no ratio.

The performance index is 100 + 25 × log2((value + 1) / (dataset median + 1)). Its median baseline includes every valid point in the chart and remains fixed during zooming. Index trend lines average index values. Raw counts remain in tooltips and the data table; cumulative counts depend on video age.

    How unusual values are identified

    For each video, compare ln(value + 1) with the median of the previous 20 plotted videos, excluding itself. The modified z-score is 0.6745 × (log value − median) / MAD. An absolute score above 3.5 flags a potential outlier. The first 20 videos, zero-MAD windows and undated sequences have no score. Flags describe unusual performance, not manipulation. Cumulative counts are affected by video age.
